Questions, answered

What is Hey100?
A match-making app for developers. You list your product with screenshots and prompts, say hey to apps you'd use, and when someone says hey back you match and trade: you use theirs, they use yours.
Who is it for?
Indie builders and small teams with a real product and fewer than about 100 active users. iOS, Android, web apps, SaaS, browser extensions and AI tools all count.
Do I need a live product?
You need something people can use, even if it's in TestFlight or beta. Every account lists a product, which is what makes every match a fair trade.
What's a trade?
Once you match, both sides get each other's test links and codes. You each use the other app and send short, structured feedback: first impression, what confused you, what you'd pay.
How is this different from feedback swap sites?
No credits or queues. Both sides have to want to try each other's app before a chat opens, so the people testing your app actually chose it.
Is it free?
Your first 5 matches are free. After that, Pro unlocks unlimited heys and matches, better visibility and full analytics. Inviting a builder gets you both 3 more matches.
Why invite-only?
A match-making app only works when there are enough good matches. We are letting builders in by category, in waves, so each group has plenty of apps to find.
What do you verify?
Optionally connect GitHub, App Store Connect, Stripe or RevenueCat to show badges like "shipped", "active" or a revenue band. Exact numbers are never shown unless you choose to.
