Who we are

Hey100 is operated by How They Grow LLC ("Hey100", "we", "us"). We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

You can reach us about anything in this policy at hey@hey100.com.

What this covers

This policy applies to the hey100.com website, including the waitlist, and to the Hey100 iOS app. Where something applies to only one of them, we say so.

What we collect

If you join the waitlist on the website

  • Your email address. This is the only thing we ask you for.
  • A referral code. We generate this from the part of your email address before the @, so that your invite link is memorable. This means your referral link may contain your email username — for example hey100.com/w/alex. It never contains your full email address or your domain. If you would rather have an anonymous code, email us and we will change it.
  • Referral activity. Who referred you, if anyone, and how many people you have referred, so we can move you up the list.
  • When you joined, which is what determines your position.
  • Your IP address, recorded when you join so we can rate-limit signups and block abuse. It is held in a table separate from your waitlist entry and is never linked to your email address, used to profile you, or shown to anyone.
  • Local storage in your browser holding your own waitlist position, so the page remembers you. This is not a cookie, is not sent to us, and is not readable by anyone else.

The website runs no analytics, no advertising pixels and no tracking cookies. We do not know who visits, only who signs up.

If you create an account in the app

  • Sign-in identity. You can sign in with Apple, email, Google or GitHub. We receive a user identifier and, depending on the method, your name, email address and profile picture. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we only ever see Apple's private relay address and cannot see your real one.
  • Your profile — display name, avatar, and anything else you choose to add.
  • Your product listing — product name, description, category, screenshots, prompts, links, and any test links, TestFlight invitations or promo codes you choose to share with people you match with.
  • Matching activity — the apps you say hey to, who says hey back, and your matches.
  • Messages and feedback you send through the app after a match. We store these so the conversation works. We do not read them except where we genuinely need to — investigating a report of abuse, or where the law requires it.
  • Subscription status — whether you have Pro, and when it renews.
  • Device and diagnostic data — device model, operating system version, app version, and crash and error reports, used to keep the app working.
  • Push notification token, if you turn notifications on, so we can tell you about new heys, matches and messages. You can turn this off at any time in iOS Settings.

If you connect a verification service

Connecting GitHub, App Store Connect, Stripe or RevenueCat is entirely optional. Hey100 works without it. If you do connect one, we receive an access token and read a narrow set of figures from it, such as commit counts, active user counts or revenue totals.

We use those figures to show badges and ranges — "shipped", "active", or a revenue band like $2–3k/mo. Your exact numbers are never shown to other users unless you explicitly choose to reveal them. You can disconnect any service at any time, which revokes our token and removes the badge.

We never receive your Stripe balance, payout details, customer list or any individual transaction — only the aggregate figures needed to produce a badge.

Payments

Pro is sold as an auto-renewing subscription through the Apple App Store. Apple processes the payment, not us. We never see or store your card number, billing address or Apple Account credentials. We receive only a confirmation that a valid subscription exists and when it renews or lapses.

How we use it

  • To run the waitlist and tell you when your wave opens.
  • To create and operate your account.
  • To show your product to other builders and to find you sensible matches.
  • To deliver messages and feedback between matched builders.
  • To show verification badges you have opted into.
  • To process and recognise your Pro subscription.
  • To keep the service safe — rate limiting, spam and fraud prevention, and acting on reports.
  • To fix bugs and improve the product.
  • To send you service emails you cannot opt out of while you hold an account, such as security notices, and product emails you can opt out of at any time.

Legal bases

If you are in the UK, EU or EEA, we rely on these legal bases under the UK GDPR and GDPR:

  • Performance of a contract — running your account, matching, messaging and your subscription.
  • Legitimate interests — keeping the service secure, preventing abuse, understanding and fixing faults, and operating the waitlist. We have considered your rights and do not think these uses override them.
  • Consent — optional verification connections, push notifications, and marketing email. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation — where we must keep or disclose data by law.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers, each handling data on our instructions:

  • Vercel — website and API hosting.
  • Neon — the database holding waitlist and account records.
  • Resend — transactional email. A signup notification containing your email address is sent to us when you join the waitlist.
  • Apple — app distribution, Sign in with Apple, push notifications and subscription billing.
  • Google and GitHub — only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • Stripe, RevenueCat, GitHub and App Store Connect — only if you choose to connect them for verification.

Two third parties are reachable from the website itself:

  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces, which discloses your IP address to Google when the page loads.
  • TikTok hosts the founder video embedded on the home page. If you play it, TikTok may set its own cookies and receive your IP address under TikTok's own privacy policy. The video does not autoplay with sound and nothing is sent to TikTok until the embed loads.

We may also disclose data if we are legally required to, or to protect the rights and safety of our users. If Hey100 is ever acquired, data may transfer as part of that transaction, and we will tell you before it does.

What we never do

  • We never sell your personal data, and we never "share" it for cross-context behavioural advertising as the CPRA defines that term.
  • We do not run advertising networks or ad tracking in the app or on the site.
  • We do not show your exact revenue, user counts or commit counts to anyone unless you choose to reveal them.
  • We do not read your matched conversations except to investigate an abuse report or where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Waitlist entries — until you ask us to remove you, or until the waitlist is retired.
  • Rate-limiting IP records — held separately for abuse prevention and never linked to your email address or your waitlist entry.
  • Account data — while your account is open, then deleted within 30 days of you deleting it.
  • Messages — deleted with your account. A copy may remain visible to the person you matched with, since it is their conversation too.
  • Billing records — retained as long as tax and accounting law requires, typically seven years.
  • Backups — deleted data may persist in encrypted backups for up to 90 days before being overwritten.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, or export it in a portable format. Email hey@hey100.com and we will respond within 30 days.

If you are in the UK, EU or EEA, you additionally have the right to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local supervisory authority.

If you are in California, you have the right to know what we collect, to delete it, to correct it, and to opt out of sale or sharing — though as above, we do neither. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

Deleting your account

You can delete your Hey100 account and all its data from inside the app, under Settings → Account → Delete Account. You do not need to email us or ask permission. If you would rather we did it, email hey@hey100.com from your account address.

Deleting your account removes your profile, product listing, matches and messages. An active Pro subscription is managed by Apple and is not cancelled by deleting your account — cancel it in your Apple Account settings, or it will keep renewing.

To leave the waitlist, email us and we will delete your entry.

International transfers

We are based in the United States and our providers operate globally, so your data is processed in the US and potentially elsewhere. Where data leaves the UK, EU or EEA, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK Addendum, or an adequacy decision, depending on the provider.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit with TLS. Data is encrypted at rest by our hosting and database providers. Access to production data is limited to people who need it. Admin endpoints require a secret key and are rate limited.

No service is perfectly secure. If we ever discover a breach affecting your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant regulator as the law requires.

Children

Hey100 is for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us data, email hey@hey100.com and we will delete it promptly. If you are between 13 and the age of digital consent where you live, please use Hey100 with a parent or guardian's involvement.

Changes

We will update this policy as Hey100 grows. The date at the top always reflects the current version. If a change materially affects your rights, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
